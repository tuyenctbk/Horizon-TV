Privacy Policy

Horizon TV Broadcast Weather Platform • Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Horizon TV. We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any personal data processed through our application. This Privacy Policy outlines how Horizon TV collects, uses, and safeguards information when you utilize our 24/7 meteorological broadcast platform, live Doppler radar, and weather telemetry services.

2. Information We Collect

Horizon TV is designed with a privacy-first approach. We collect only the minimum necessary data to provide hyper-local weather forecasting and interactive radar features:

  • Location Data (Coarse and Fine Location): If granted permission, Horizon TV accesses your device location to retrieve current weather conditions, severe weather alerts, and center the Doppler radar map on your area. Location data is queried directly against open forecasting APIs (such as Open-Meteo) and is not stored or tracked on external servers.
  • Local Application Preferences: Your saved settings—such as favorited forecast cities, unit systems (Celsius/Fahrenheit), audio options, and CRT visual preferences—are stored exclusively on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences).
  • Device Information & Technical Telemetry: Standard non-identifying technical data (such as OS version and device model) may be processed locally to optimize broadcast video rendering, scale layout dimensions for TV/tablet screens, and handle crash reports.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To deliver accurate, real-time meteorological forecasts, radar imagery, and emergency storm warnings.
  • To customize regional broadcast widgets and L-bar telemetry overlays based on your selected location.
  • To maintain application performance, prevent technical crashes, and improve broadcast stream stability.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Horizon TV does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. To deliver meteorological feeds and platform functionality, we interface with trusted open API endpoints:

  • Open-Meteo API: Used for global weather telemetry, diurnal forecasts, and air quality index calculations.
  • Google Play Services: Used for application updates, installation verification, and Android TV operational support.

5. Data Retention & Control

You maintain full control over your data within Horizon TV:

  • Location Access: You can grant or revoke location permissions at any time through your Android device settings.
  • Clearing Saved Preferences: You can clear all favorited stations and custom settings directly from the application settings menu or by clearing the app's cache/data in Android system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Horizon TV does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.
